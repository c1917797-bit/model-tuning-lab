# Page Roles And Template Selection

Use this reference before creating the outline or choosing page types.

## Cover

- Opens the report.
- Keep title, subtitle, date, and source editable.
- Avoid complex decoration.

## Agenda / Section

- Use a normal agenda format.
- One chapter title per line.
- Mark the current chapter in red.
- Keep agenda items cohesive; do not mix different levels of granularity.
- A useful structure:
  - `01 Overview`
  - `02 Core Capability Insights`
    - `2.1 AI for Apps`
    - `2.2 Apps for AI`
    - `2.3 Performance`
    - `2.4 Graphics and Imaging`
    - `2.5 Cross-Device`
  - `03 Opportunity Analysis`

## One-Page Overview

- Use this near the start to explain why the event, technology, or finding matters.
- Do not spend the page saying "what we observed".
- Do not state common knowledge.
- Use 4-6 key-change cards plus one main-line conclusion.

## Technology Stack / Capability Map

- Use layered architecture, capability maps, or five-layer technology-stack diagrams.
- Best for AI stacks, OS capability layers, and developer-platform layers.

## Mechanism Breakdown

- Use flow diagrams or call-chain diagrams.
- Best for explaining how a capability works.

## Left Image + Right Insight Cards

- Use a large official screenshot, product UI, or source image on the left.
- Use three numbered insight cards on the right.
- Best when an official screenshot or product interface is available.

## Competitive Matrix / Opportunity Table

- Prefer editable PPT tables when text will likely be revised.
- Good columns include capability, platform change, planning direction, and department opportunity.
- Do not add a priority column unless the user asks for it.
- Use red only for key phrases.

## Chapter Synthesis

- Use this at the end of a chapter to explain implications, opportunities, or planning direction.
- Do not force opportunities into every page.
- Opportunity discussion is usually more natural at chapter endings.

## Final Summary

- Use 3-4 final judgments.
- Do not pack every detail into the final page.
- The bottom conclusion should be memorable but restrained.
