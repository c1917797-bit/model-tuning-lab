# Technology Company Internal Report Style

## Base Style

- Use a white background as the default.
- Use red or black-red title treatments, but keep them restrained.
- Use red only for titles, key differences, key capabilities, numbering, and small emphasis.
- Use black or dark gray for body text.
- Use optional blue for neutral capability blocks and system modules.
- Use thin lines, compact grids, and low-radius cards.
- Use medium-high information density, but keep the structure clear.
- Middle visuals should look like formal internal report pages, not web posters, AI ads, or script-drawn block diagrams.

## Titles

- Put the viewpoint in the title.
- Prefer short assertion titles over generic topic labels.
- Reading only the titles should reveal the deck storyline.
- Prefer the pattern `Object: clear change / mechanism / conclusion`.
- Good title examples:
  - `Foundation Models: System models open to third-party apps through Generation API`
  - `App Intents dual schema: Third-party capabilities enter the system AI call chain`
  - `Xcode Agent: From code completion to goal-driven optimization`
  - `MetricKit / Instruments: Performance governance forms an observe-locate-fix-verify loop`

## Typography

- Chinese: Microsoft YaHei.
- English and numbers: Arial or Aptos.
- Use at most three practical font sizes: title, body/card, caption/table.
- Use short labels, short sentences, and structured modules.
- Avoid long paragraphs inside the middle canvas.
- Avoid scripture-like title patterns such as "not X, but Y".
- Avoid stiff labels such as "evidence map", "management summary", "opportunity roadmap", or "page outline" unless the user explicitly asks for them.
- Avoid AI-flavored slogans such as "reshape the future ecosystem" or "paradigm revolution".

## Avoid

- Marketing hero pages unless explicitly requested.
- Decorative gradients, cartoon visuals, generic stock images.
- Speaker-only conference screenshots.
- Large dark backgrounds.
- Red warning boxes, red lightbulb icons, exclamation-style risk icons, or alarm styling.
- Excessive red.
- Overuse of left red vertical bars; do not use a left red vertical bar by default.
- Raw Excel-looking tables.
